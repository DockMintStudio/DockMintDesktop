How this prototype works

This is the DockMint desktop app. It stores only its own workspace data.

  • Switch workspaces in the left sidebar.
  • Use Customize workspace to change colors, layout, and visible panels.
  • Drag a panel card to change its order.
  • Press Ctrl + K to search sample files and tasks.
  • Tasks, notes, and customization are saved locally and remain after restarting DockMint.
  • This version cannot browse, open, edit, or delete real files, and it does not launch applications.
